Activity 1 — Elements of Culture
⏱ 6 min 3 / 10
🎤
Teacher says

"In your group, complete the table comparing Emirati culture with another culture you know. Click each element to see examples!"

🌐 Click each cultural element below to explore it!

👗

Dress & Appearance

👆 Click to explore

Emirati Culture: Kandura (men), Abaya (women), Ghutra & Agal headwear — representing modesty, identity and heritage.

Other Cultures: Traditional dress varies hugely — from Kilts in Scotland to Sarees in India to Hanbok in Korea. All carry deep cultural meaning!
🗣️

Communication & Language

👆 Click to explore

Emirati Culture: Arabic is the official language. Greetings like "As-salamu alaykum" reflect Islamic values and respect.

Key Fact: Africa alone has over 2,000 languages — about one-third of all languages in the world, making it the most linguistically diverse continent!
🍽️

Food & Eating Habits

👆 Click to explore

Emirati Culture: Machboos, harees, luqaimat, dates and camel milk — food is deeply connected to celebration, hospitality and family.

Other Cultures: Japan has sushi & ramen, Mexico has tacos & mole, Ethiopia has injera — food tells the story of a people's history and land.
🕌

Beliefs & Values

👆 Click to explore

Emirati Culture: Islam is central to Emirati values — hospitality (karam), respect for elders, community, and generosity are core values.

Other Cultures: Hinduism, Buddhism, Christianity, Judaism and many other belief systems shape the values and daily practices of billions of people worldwide.
🎨

Arts & Literature

👆 Click to explore

Emirati Culture: Arabic calligraphy, Al-Ayyala dance, falconry, poetry and traditional music (Al Taghrooda) are UNESCO-recognised heritage arts.

Other Cultures: From Flamenco in Spain to Kabuki in Japan, every culture expresses itself through unique art forms that carry centuries of history.

Understanding different cultures helps us acknowledge our similarities and respect our differences. As the textbook says: "The more we know and understand about other cultures, the easier it is for us to show them tolerance and respect."

🎓 Teacher NoteStudents can discuss which element surprised them most. Time: 8 min

🎪 Do events that celebrate multiculturalism promote tolerance and help people overcome their prejudices?

🌍 Here are some examples from the UAE and around the world. Click each one!

🌍

UAE Cultural Festivals

👆 Click to explore

Events like Dubai Food Festival, Global Village and Hatta Heritage Village bring people from 200+ nationalities together. Sharing food, music, dance and crafts helps break down barriers and replace fear with curiosity and respect.
🎭

School Cultural Days

👆 Click to explore

When schools hold International Days — where students wear traditional dress, bring food and share stories — students learn that differences are fascinating, not frightening. Research shows students become more open-minded after such events.
🏅

International Tolerance Day

👆 Click to explore

Observed on November 16 every year, this global day promotes awareness of the dangers of intolerance and the importance of mutual respect. The UAE's Tolerance Bridge was named on this very day!

1️⃣ What does "tolerance" mean?

🅐 Agreeing with everyone's views and opinions
🅑 Accepting and respecting people who are different from you
🅒 Ignoring differences and treating everyone the same
🅓 Only mixing with people from your own culture

2️⃣ What is a "snap judgment"?

🅐 A very careful and well-thought-out decision
🅑 A judgement made after getting to know someone well
🅒 A quick opinion formed before getting to know someone
🅓 A judgement based on lots of evidence and research

3️⃣ According to the text, why did people LONG AGO develop prejudice?

🅐 Because they were naturally cruel people
🅑 Because they rarely travelled or met people from other places
🅒 Because they read too many newspapers
🅓 Because they had too much free time

4️⃣ What is the UDHR?

🅐 A cultural festival in the UAE
🅑 A school programme for diversity
🅒 The Universal Declaration of Human Rights, adopted by the UN in 1948
🅓 A government policy about immigration

5️⃣ What does a "Whole School Approach" to diversity mean?

🅐 Only teachers need to promote tolerance
🅑 Having one lesson about diversity per year
🅒 Everyone in the school community working together to create an inclusive environment
🅓 Making all students wear the same uniform
